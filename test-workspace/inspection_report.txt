MRPL — Pressure Vessel Inspection Report
Unit: Crude Distillation Unit-2, Vessel V-204
Inspection Date: 14 August 2026
Inspector: J. Rao, NDT Level II

Observations:
1. External surface corrosion noted on the lower shell course, approximately
   0.8 mm pitting depth, localized to a 30 cm x 15 cm area near the north
   support saddle.
2. Insulation jacket found damaged at two locations, allowing moisture
   ingress — likely contributor to the corrosion above.
3. Nozzle N-4 flange bolting shows minor surface rust; torque check within
   acceptable range.
4. No indications on UT thickness survey exceeding the corrosion allowance;
   minimum measured thickness 11.2 mm against a design minimum of 9.5 mm.
5. Relief valve PSV-204A test date is current; next due March 2027.

No active leaks observed. Vessel remains fit for continued service, but the
insulation damage should be addressed before the next monsoon season to
prevent further moisture-driven corrosion at the affected location.
